# Threat Hunting Blogs

### The Frontline of Digital Defense: Top Threat Hunting and Security Blogs

In the digital age, where cyber threats evolve at an unprecedented pace, staying informed is not just beneficial—it's critical for survival. For cybersecurity professionals, enthusiasts, and organizations, threat hunting blogs are valuable resources for gaining insights, best practices, and the latest trends. Here's a compilation of premier threat hunting blogs and additional resources that should be on your radar.

#### Microsoft Security Blog

[Microsoft Security Blog](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/) offers insights and best practices directly from one of the tech industry's giants. It's an essential read for those who want to understand the landscape of digital threats and Microsoft's role in combating them.

#### CrowdStrike Blog

The [CrowdStrike Blog](https://www.crowdstrike.com/blog/) provides expert analysis and reports on cybersecurity threats. With a focus on threat intelligence and endpoint security, this blog is invaluable for hunters looking to stay a step ahead of cyber adversaries.

#### Palo Alto Networks Blog

[Palo Alto Networks Blog](https://www.paloaltonetworks.com/blog/) covers a broad spectrum of topics from cloud security to AI in cybersecurity. It's a great source for diverse content that touches on all aspects of security in the digital space.

#### Check Point Software Blog

The [Check Point Software Blog](https://blog.checkpoint.com/) offers deep dives into malware analysis, threat prevention, and security research, making it a treasure trove for those interested in the technical side of cybersecurity.

#### Cisco Security Blogs

[Cisco's Security Blogs](https://blogs.cisco.com/security) provide thought leadership and strategic insights on building a robust security posture, emphasizing network security.

#### Cybereason Blog

The [Cybereason Blog](https://www.cybereason.com/blog) gives an analytical perspective on cybersecurity news and trends, providing detailed analysis and cybersecurity advice.

#### SentinelOne Blog

The [SentinelOne Blog](https://www.sentinelone.com/blog/) features posts from a cybersecurity company at the forefront of AI-driven threat detection and response, offering cutting-edge insights.

#### Wazuh Blog

The [Wazuh Blog](https://wazuh.com/blog/) is an open-source security platform's repository of insights, focusing on intrusion detection, monitoring, and compliance.

#### BlackBerry Blog

The [BlackBerry Blog](https://blogs.blackberry.com/en/home) discusses enterprise security and endpoint management, reflecting on BlackBerry's pivot to a security software and services company.

#### IBM Security Community Blogs

[IBM Security Community Blogs](https://community.ibm.com/community/user/security/participate/blogs) invite readers to explore various cybersecurity topics contributed by a community of experts and practitioners.

#### F5 Corporate Blog

The \[F5 Corporate Blog]\(https

://[www.f5.com/company/blog](http://www.f5.com/company/blog)) dives into network performance and security, offering articles that discuss protecting applications across multiple environments.

#### Infoblox Company Blogs

Infoblox's [Company Blogs](https://blogs.infoblox.com/) provide a window into the world of secure cloud-managed network services, emphasizing DNS security.

#### Azure Security Blog

The [Azure Security Blog](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/blog/topics/security/) is Microsoft's cloud-centric resource for those invested in securing Azure environments and understanding cloud security landscapes.

#### AWS Security Blog

Amazon Web Services' [Security Blog](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/security/) is a must-follow for professionals using AWS infrastructure, offering guidance on protecting your AWS resources.

#### Google Cloud Security Blog

[Google Cloud Security Blog](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/developers-practitioners/google-cloud-security-overview) provides insights on securing Google Cloud deployments and includes resources for developers and practitioners.

#### Splunk Blogs

Splunk's [Blogs](https://www.splunk.com/en_us/blog) turn data analytics into actionable security intelligence, offering a data-centric approach to threat hunting and response.

#### SumoLogic Resources

[SumoLogic's Resources](https://www.sumologic.com/resources/) offer a collection of articles, research papers, and reports on continuous intelligence and insights from machine data.

#### LogRhythm Security Blog

The [LogRhythm Blog](https://logrhythm.com/blog/) covers topics from compliance to advanced threat detection, emphasizing the importance of a strong security analytics platform.

#### MITRE ATT\&CK® on Medium

[MITRE ATT\&CK® on Medium](https://medium.com/mitre-attack) is a user-friendly platform for understanding the tactics, techniques, and procedures used by threat actors, straight from the creators of the ATT\&CK framework.

#### Additional Recommendations:

* **The Hacker News**: Offers a wide array of cybersecurity news, including data breaches, vulnerabilities, and hacker information.
* **Krebs on Security**: Run by journalist Brian Krebs, this blog is a leading source for investigative stories on cybercrime and internet security.
* **Dark Reading**: A cybersecurity news site that covers threats, vulnerabilities, and technology trends.
* **Schneier on Security**: Bruce Schneier's blog provides expert insights into security, privacy, and cryptography.

#### Conclusion

In the dynamic field of cybersecurity, staying updated through these blogs and resources is not a choice, but a necessity. These platforms not only educate and inform but also foster a community of knowledge sharing that's vital for collective cyber resilience. Whether you're a seasoned professional or a curious newcomer, these blogs are your windows into the vast and ever-changing realm of cybersecurity.
